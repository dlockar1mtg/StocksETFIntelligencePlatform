## Mission

The Stocks and ETF Intelligence Platform will independently collect, validate,
analyze, forecast, rank, and explain public-market investment opportunities.

The architecture will support both exchange-traded funds and individual
equities. The first certified production scope will be limited to approved ETFs.

The platform will publish governed and reproducible outputs to the Universal
Investment Platform for cross-asset allocation and portfolio decision support.

## Initial Certified-Scope Candidates

The initial portfolio ETF universe is:

- VOO
- SCHD
- QQQM

These securities are candidates for future certification. Their inclusion in
this charter does not by itself authorize production forecasts,
recommendations, allocations, or UIP imports.

## Authority Boundary

### Stocks and ETF Intelligence Platform

The domain platform owns:

- Domain source collection
- Source reconciliation
- Security identity
- Corporate-action processing
- Return reconstruction
- ETF analytics
- Domain forecasting
- Risk analysis
- Domain ranking
- Portfolio-action recommendations
- Stocks and ETF contribution-allocation recommendations
- Domain validation and certification
- Governed export-package generation

### Universal Investment Platform

The UIP owns:

- Universal contract validation
- Transactional package import
- Cross-domain normalization
- Cross-asset comparison
- Total-portfolio risk budgeting
- Cross-domain ranking
- Final allocation across investment domains
- Universal decision reporting

### Owner Authority

Devon Lockard retains final authority over:

- Investment approval
- Capital deployment
- Brokerage activity
- Trade execution
- Expansion of production scope
- Activation of individual-stock analysis

## Prohibited Actions

The platform shall not:

- Execute trades automatically
- Initiate brokerage transfers
- Write directly to UIP production data
- Bypass UIP package validation
- Treat research outputs as certified outputs
- Generate certified decisions from stale, conflicted, or blocked evidence
- Expand into individual-stock production analysis without separate approval
- Represent forecasts as guarantees

## Operating Cadence

The governed target cadence is:

- Daily after-close market monitoring
- Weekly analytical recalculation
- Monthly certified decision package
- Event-driven certified reruns only through governed triggers or manual authorization

## Forecast Horizons

The governed horizons are:

- 1 month
- 3 months
- 1 year
- 3 years
- 5 years

The 3-year horizon is the primary strategic ETF decision horizon.

## Integration Standard

The platform shall communicate with the UIP through versioned, immutable,
manifest-governed export packages.

Direct writes to UIP production tables are prohibited.

## Certification Limitation

Completion of repository scaffolding, tests, or pipeline execution does not
authorize investment recommendations.

Production authority requires the approved layered certification process.
