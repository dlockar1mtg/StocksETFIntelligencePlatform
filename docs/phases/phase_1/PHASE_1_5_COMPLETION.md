# Phase 1.5 — Integrated Data Foundation Certification

## Purpose

Certify the Phase 1 market-data foundation as one governed system after completion of provider contracts, raw acquisition, point-in-time normalization, and evidence-quality controls.

## Integrated controls

Phase 1.5 verifies:

- exact security identity for VOO, SCHD, and QQQM;
- consistency of all ten governed data domains across provider and quality controls;
- immutable raw capture and raw-data exclusion from Git;
- mandatory point-in-time fields and look-ahead prevention;
- provider-adjusted reconciliation and corporate-action evidence;
- missing-evidence confidence penalties;
- conflict preservation and quarantine;
- a cumulative regression floor of 106 tests;
- no expansion into certified monitoring, analytics, recommendations, UIP export, database writes, or execution.

## Authority

A passing Phase 1 certification establishes a governed data-foundation implementation only. It does not certify live providers or production market monitoring and grants no analytical or decision authority.
